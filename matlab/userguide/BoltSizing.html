SINGLE JOINT

Bolt Sizing

Gut-check bolt capability across the library's sizes before defining the joint. Bolt strength only: no preload, nut, insert, bearing or slip.

Fields

Loads and material

FieldWhat it sets
Bolt materialOne material for the whole sweep. Required.
Limit tension (lbf)PtL on the most-loaded bolt.
Limit shear (lbf)PsL on the most-loaded bolt, per shear plane.
Shear planeThreads or body. Sets the shear area and the interaction exponents (5020B Eq. 20–23).

What you'll see

  • Size bolts stays disabled until a material and at least one load are entered.
  • Nothing here is saved in the case, and editing never marks it unsaved.
  • Use this size → sends the bolt and loads to Joint Config, even from a failing row. Joint Config gives the real answer.
