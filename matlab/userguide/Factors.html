SETUP

Factors

Set the fitting factor and the four factors of safety your program levies. They apply to every analysis in the case.

Fields

Fitting Factor

FieldWhat it sets
Fitting factor (FF)One factor multiplying every factor of safety: ultimate, yield, separation and slip (5020B §4.2.2). A minimum of 1.15 is recommended for ultimate.

Factors of Safety

FieldWhat it sets
Yield (FSy)Yield factor of safety.
Ultimate (FSu)Ultimate factor of safety.
Separation (FSsep)Separation factor of safety.
Slip (FSslip)Slip factor of safety.

What you'll see

  • The engine keeps four fitting factors (ultimate, yield, separation, slip); this page shows one.
  • A loaded case with unequal fitting factors keeps them, and a banner says so. Editing FF replaces all four with the one value.
  • The tool warns when FF is below 1.15 on a separation-critical joint.
  • A new case starts at FF 1.15, FSu 1.4, FSy 1.25, FSsep 1.0, FSslip 1.0. Check them against your program's factors.
