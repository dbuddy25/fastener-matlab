SETUP

Factors

Set the fitting factors and factors of safety your program levies, one pair per check. They apply to every analysis in the case.

Fields

Fitting Factors

FieldWhat it sets
Yield (FFy)Multiplies FSy (5020B §4.2.2).
Ultimate (FFu)Multiplies FSu. A minimum of 1.15 is recommended.
Separation (FFsep)Multiplies FSsep. At least 1.15 on a separation-critical joint.
Slip (FFslip)Multiplies FSslip.

Factors of Safety

FieldWhat it sets
Yield (FSy)Yield factor of safety.
Ultimate (FSu)Ultimate factor of safety.
Separation (FSsep)Separation factor of safety.
Slip (FSslip)Slip factor of safety.

What you'll see

  • Each check's design load uses its own pair: e.g. yield is FFy × FSy × PtL. A program that levies the fitting factor on ultimate only sets FFu 1.15 and the rest 1.0.
  • The tool warns when FFsep is below 1.15 on a separation-critical joint.
  • A new case starts with all four fitting factors at 1.15, and FSu 1.4, FSy 1.25, FSsep 1.0, FSslip 1.0. Check them against your program's factors.
  • The summary bar at the bottom shows unequal fitting factors as FF mixed, with all four values.
