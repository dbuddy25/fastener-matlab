SINGLE JOINT

Joint Config

Define the joint: bolt, washers, flange stack, threaded member, preload and loads. Then press Analyze Single Joint.

Fields

Bolt

Joint nameA label. Never analysed.

Bolt materialRequired.
Bolt count nfFasteners in the pattern.

Washer under bolt head

Washer specA washer family. Picking a size fills and locks OD, ID and thickness. Custom unlocks them.

Threaded member

FieldWhat it sets
TypeWhat the bolt threads into: Nut, Helical Insert or Tapped Hole.
Nut specA nut family, resolved at this bolt's thread. Fills and locks material and engagement. Custom unlocks them. Nut only.
Nut materialThe material carrying the internal thread. Reads Parent (host) material for an insert or tapped hole.
Engagement (x bolt D)Insert engagement as a multiple of bolt diameter, e.g. 1.5. Helical Insert only.

Nut rated ultimate (lbf)Nut: the spec-rated ultimate, which is the nut's ultimate allowable (5020B §4.4.1). Insert (Rated pull-out): the specified internal-thread allowable. Blank = no rating.

Bolt length

FieldWhat it sets
Overall bolt length (in)Under-head to tip. Pick a catalogued length or type any other. Blank = the 5020B §4.7.4 estimate (grip + nut height + 2 × pitch).

Advanced / overrides

FieldWhat it sets
Unthreaded body length L1 (in)Shank length inside the clamp, for bolt stiffness. Blank = derived from bolt length and catalogue thread length.
Bolt rated ultimate (lbf)Spec-rated Ptu-allow. Blank = At × Ftu.
Bolt rated yield (lbf)Spec-rated Pty-allow. Blank = 5020B Eq. 18.
Frustum half-angle (deg)Member-stiffness cone half-angle. Default 30.
Thermal preload rate (lbf/°C)Preload change per °C from assembly, applied + on maximum and − on minimum preload. Blank = derived from the bolt and stack CTEs (TM-106943 Eq. 10). A rate in lbf/°F × 1.8 is lbf/°C.

Analysis assumptions

FieldWhat it sets
Shear planeThreads or body. Sets the shear area (Eq. 12 / 13) and interaction exponents (Eq. 20–23). Threads is conservative.
Slip modeSingle fastener (Eq. 86) or joint (Eq. 84).
Friction coefficientFaying-surface friction. Zero = slip not evaluated.
Bolt axisFE axis the bolt lies along. Bulk runs use it to split element forces into tension and shear.
Loading-plane factor nn = Llp / L. 1.0 is conservative.
Bolt bending (4.4.4)Records the 5020B §4.4.4 exemption when no moment is entered. Accounted for (clearance or gap) leaves Interaction not evaluated until a moment is supplied.

What you'll see

  • Required dropdowns start blank. A material is never picked for you.
  • Picking a nut or washer spec fills and locks the dependent fields. Custom unlocks them. If the spec has no match at this bolt size it reverts to Custom and the status bar says why.
  • Changing the bolt re-resolves the nut and both washer specs.
  • Engagement is two fields: a ratio for an insert, a length for a nut or tapped hole. Changing the type greys out the one that no longer applies; it keeps its value but is not used. A ratio is never read as inches.
  • The Bolt length readout itemises grip, minimum length and which L1 is in force, and updates as you edit.
  • Analyze Single Joint lists what is still missing rather than refusing silently. See Required before Analyze below.
  • View Cross-Section works on a half-built joint and redraws as you edit.
  • Save to Defined Joints adds the joint to this case for bulk runs. Names collide ignoring case: JT-A overwrites jt-a after a confirm.
  • Advanced / overrides starts collapsed.

Required before Analyze

GroupRequired
BoltBolt, Bolt material
Threaded memberNut material (Parent material for an insert or tapped hole)
Flange stackAt least one layer thickness; for every layer with a thickness, its Material and Edge
WashersWasher material, when the washer is present
PreloadNominal torque
Applied loadsAt least one limit load (a bending moment alone counts)

Everything else is optional: a blank input leaves only the checks that need it not evaluated.
