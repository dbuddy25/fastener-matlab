ABOUT THE TOOL

What the tool does not do

Know these before relying on a clean table.

Gaps in the 5020B assessment

Not coveredWhat to do instead
Yield and separation under combined loading (5020B §4.4.4, TFSR 11)Assess separately. Only ultimate interaction is implemented.
Deriving bolt bending from shearSupply the limit moment on Joint Config. With a clearance or gapped joint and no moment, Interaction reads Not evaluated.
Plastic bending (5020B Eq. 21/23)Not implemented.
Fatigue (5020B §4.5)Out of scope.
Thread-form compatibility, fillet clearance, locking-feature engagement, runout, blind-hole bottoming (5020B §4.7)Check by hand against the drawing.

Modelling limits

LimitEffect
One bolt, the most loaded, not a patternEnter that bolt's loads. Joint-level slip takes the joint totals you supply.
Mixed-modulus flange stacks use an averaged member modulusExact for an ordinary two-plate joint. With soft layers at both bearing faces, joint stiffness can read high and φ low, which is unconservative for bolt tension.
A threaded-in joint missing frustum geometryFalls back to φ = 1 (conservative).

Decisions that stay yours

  • Whether friction may be relied on.
  • Whether the joint is separation-critical.
  • The factors your program levies.

The tool warns where an entry sits outside what 5020B permits without test substantiation.

Units

US customary (in, lbf, psi). Temperatures are in °C.
