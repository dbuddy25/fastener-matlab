ABOUT THE TOOL

Where the numbers come from

Which document governs

RoleDocumentsCited when
GoverningNASA-STD-5020BWherever it gives the equation
SupplementalNASA TM-106943, NASA RP-1228Only where 5020B relies on them for a formula it does not print (thermal preload change, bearing, tear-out, thread shear)
Hardware dataNAS / NASM specification sheets, vendor bulletinsLibrary allowables and geometry, cited per entry
Stiffness method30° conical-frustum method (Shigley); SAND2008-0371 and NASA TM-108377 as backgroundBolt and joint stiffness (kb, kc, φ). 5020B takes these as given inputs to Eq. 9 and prints no method. All three are listed in Help > References.

Every margin's Method names the document and equation number that produced it.

The documents themselves

Help > References... lists every document the tool's numbers rest on, its role, and what the tool takes from it.

Most are copyrighted and do not ship with the tool. The citation is always there. Point the References window at your own folder of standards to open your copies.

Library values

Every material and hardware entry carries its source: standard, table and assumption. Materials & Hardware shows it for every row. Check it first when a number is not what you expected.
